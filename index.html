Cutting edge technology

Technology that most mold shops cannot offer

TK invests in the processes that decide part quality and cost, so your program launches faster and runs cleaner at volume.

Conformal cooling

3D printed conformal cooling channels follow the part geometry for up to 30 percent higher cooling efficiency, shorter cycles, and less warpage.

Rapid heat cycle molding

RHCM delivers high gloss, weld line free surfaces and reliable thin walls on cosmetic parts, with fewer stress marks and secondary steps.

Lean automation

Automated cells handle in mold placement, assembly, inspection, and secondary processing for consistent quality at production volume.
