## Suggested use

- Short clips (servo unscrewing, 2K, stack molds, in mold closing) are ideal for X (@brandontkmold) and LinkedIn, showing motion that static renders cannot.
- The 128 cavity tube, AP stack mold, and 2K mold clips are the strongest "wow" assets for cold outreach to packaging and medical OEMs.
- Pair each clip with the matching case study spec from `04` or `05`.
