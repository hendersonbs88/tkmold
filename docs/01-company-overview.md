# TK Group (Holdings) Limited: Company Overview

> Synthesized from TK marketing decks, the internal TK Mold brief, and the US business plan. Source of truth for the public-facing company profile.

## Who TK Is

TK Group (Holdings) Limited (Hong Kong Stock Exchange: 2283.HK) is a precision injection mold manufacturer and contract molder. The company designs and builds high cavitation, tight tolerance tooling and runs production molding across China and Vietnam.

- **Public ticker:** 2283.HK (Hong Kong listed)
- **Brand name in market:** TK Holdings / TK Group (Chinese: 東江控股)
- **Website:** www.tkmold.com
- **Sales contact:** sales@tkmold.com
- **Tagline:** "Keeping Our Customers Successful"
- **One-stop offering:** product design, mold fabrication, production molding, and engineering support under one roof

## What TK Sells

1. **Mold fabrication (tooling).** Multicavity, tight tolerance, high speed production molds. This is the flagship capability.
2. **Production molding.** Running parts at volume in China and Vietnam.
3. **Two shot / 2K molding, insert molding, overmolding, stack molds.**
4. **Engineering support.** DFM, tooling design review, mold trials, and qualification support.

## Markets Served

- **Medical** (infusion, syringes, safety devices, laboratory consumables, diagnostics)
- **Packaging** (caps and closures, thin wall containers, stack molds, tube heads)
- **Consumer electronics**
- **Automotive**
- **Cosmetics and personal care**
- **Food and beverage**

## The North and South America Play

Brandon Henderson is TK's Technical Sales Support Manager for the Americas. The strategic wedge is **US based technical oversight for China and Vietnam production.** A US point of contact who is a journeyman tool and die maker and former global tooling engineer removes the biggest objection American OEMs have to offshore tooling: time zone gaps, language friction, and no accountable engineer on this side of the ocean.

This repo (github.com/hendersonbs88/tkmold) collects the TK initiatives, use cases, and marketing material that support that Americas effort.

## Key Differentiators

- Listed company with audited financials, not an anonymous broker shop
- One-stop design to production capability
- Demonstrated big project management: 12 molds delivered simultaneously on lab consumable programs, 68 PCR plate mold sets in two years
- Advanced process competence: servo unscrewing, full hot runner valve gate systems, conformal cooling, thin wall down to 0.25 mm, 2K
- US based technical sales engineer with 15 plus years in molding
