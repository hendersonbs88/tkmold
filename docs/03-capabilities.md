# Technical Capabilities

> Synthesized from the TK Precision Mold decks, the Medical Tooling Case deck, and the Packaging Tooling Case deck. These are demonstrated capabilities backed by delivered tooling (see the case study docs).

## Cavitation

TK routinely builds and runs high cavitation tooling. Delivered cavity counts in the case record:

- Caps and closures: 32, 48, 64, 72, 96, 128 cavity
- Medical syringe components: 48, 64, 96, 128 cavity
- Lab test tube and test cap: 128 cavity
- Toothpaste flip top cap: 128 cavity, daily output over 1 million pieces

## Gating and Hot Runner Systems

- Full hot runner valve gate (the workhorse system across medical and packaging)
- Hot side gate direct onto part for zero black spots, yellowing, or peeling (critical for medical PC and PP)
- Semi hot and hot nozzle to cold runner with sub gate
- Full hot half valve gate on large lab programs
- 90 degree and 60 degree side gating, single and multi gate

## Advanced Processes

- **2K / two shot molding.** Example: CR-III child resistant outer closure in PP plus TPE, 18 plus 18 cavity. 2K pull ring flip top cap in PP plus TPE.
- **Stack molds.** 8 plus 8, 24 plus 24, 32 plus 32, 48 plus 48 cavity. Pepsi Tropicana lid at 48 plus 48, 1000 ton. Kraft Parmesan base and lid with interchangeable hot half between base and lid.
- **Conformal cooling.** Used on actuator caps and 2K pull ring caps. Documented 22 percent cycle time reduction on the 2K pull ring cap.
- **Servo unscrewing** for threaded closures and syringe screw threads.
- **In mold closing** (closing the flip top on the mold, from top or standard).
- **Insert molding and overmolding.**

## Tolerance and Thin Wall

- Concentricity to 0.1 mm on lab test tubes
- Core insert machining tolerance 0.005 to 0.008 mm on blood filters
- Luer taper tolerance 0.03 mm
- Thin wall down to 0.25 mm (safety covers, PCR plates, PCR tubes)
- Small pin features down to 0.25 mm and 0.67 mm on blood collection needles
- Average wall thickness 0.55 mm to 0.85 mm across thin wall containers and lids

## Materials Run

PP, PC, ABS, MABS, HDPE, HIPS, PS, GPPS, PE, PP plus TPE, GPPS. Medical grade transparent PC with zero cosmetic defects is a recurring requirement TK meets.

## Project Management at Scale

- 12 molds delivered simultaneously on lab consumable programs ("12 molds as one launch")
- 68 PCR plate mold sets manufactured within two years
- 6 tool pump series program managed as one project
- Lead times documented as 10 weeks PO to shipment on caps, 16 weeks kick off to shipment on Parmesan stack molds

## Tonnage Range

Tooling delivered for presses from 80 ton (cosmetic) up to 1000 ton (Pepsi Tropicana stack mold), with the bulk of medical and packaging work in the 100 to 500 ton band.

## Regions Served (from the case record)

Asia, North America, USA, Europe, Germany, Spain, China. TK has shipped tooling that runs in plants across all of these regions.
