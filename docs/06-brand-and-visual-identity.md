## Tagline

**"Keeping Our Customers Successful"**

This appears on every deck, header, and footer. It is the central brand promise. It is customer outcome language, not capability language, and the Americas messaging should keep that spirit: TK exists to make the customer's program succeed.

## Color Palette

Derived from the official TK decks and logo. TK brand is **orange plus charcoal black on white**.

| Role | Color | Approx hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary (TK Orange) | Orange | #F07F1A (approx) | Logo "T", section titles, accent bars, footer chevrons, CTAs |
| Secondary (Charcoal) | Near black | #1A1A1A | Logo "K", body headings, wordmark |
| Neutral gray | Medium gray | #808080 | Deck angled banners, secondary blocks |
| Background | White | #FFFFFF | Slide and document backgrounds |

Notes:
- The hex values above are approximations sampled from the decks. If exact brand values are needed, pull them from the official logo art before producing print pieces.
- Section header treatment in the packaging deck uses orange uppercase titles with a thin orange underline rule (for example "CAPS AND CLOUSURE", "Stack Mold", "Thin Wall").

## Typography

- Decks use a clean sans serif for headings and body (Arial / Helvetica family in practice).
- The tagline "Keeping Our Customers Successful" is rendered in an italic script style as a signature element in headers and footers.
- For Americas digital output, a modern sans (for example Manrope or Inter) pairs well with the orange and charcoal system while staying clean and industrial.

## Writing Style Rules (apply to all TK output)

- **No em dashes or en dashes anywhere.** Use commas, periods, or restructure. This is a hard rule across all of Brandon's businesses and must be enforced at the code level on any automated publishing, not just in prompts.
- Lead with customer outcomes and proof, then capability.
- Use the delivered case studies (cavity counts, cycle times, named brands) as concrete proof rather than vague superlatives.
- US audience: plain, confident, engineer to engineer. Avoid hype.
