# Market Positioning: TK in the Americas

> The strategic argument for why a North or South American OEM should tool with TK. Use this for messaging, content, and sales conversations.

## The Core Position

**China and Vietnam precision tooling, with US based technical oversight.**

TK competes on the hardest tooling (high cavitation, tight tolerance, 2K, stack, conformal cooling) where offshore cost advantage is largest and where capability, not just price, decides the win. The Americas wedge is removing the offshore risk: an accountable US engineer who speaks the customer's language and owns the program.

## Why Brandon Is the Differentiator

The single biggest objection to offshore tooling is "who do I call, in my time zone, who actually understands molds." TK answers that with a US based Technical Sales Support Manager who is:

- Journeyman tool and die maker, 15 plus years in molding
- Former Global Tooling Engineer at Technimark (programs for P&G, Hershey's, Clorox)
- CPSP certified, Six Sigma Green Belt
- Holder of a 10 year China visa for on the ground factory oversight

This is US technical accountability bolted onto a listed Chinese manufacturer's capability and cost base.

## Tariff Context (the total cost argument)

- US steel tariffs and Section 301 duties on Chinese molds stack up (the combined duty load has run around 47 percent in recent periods).
- Even with duties, Chinese tooling for complex molds remains roughly 30 to 50 percent cheaper than domestic equivalents on the hard work.
- The Vietnam option provides a tariff alternative for customers who need to diversify country of origin.
- The honest framing for customers: do not pretend tariffs do not exist. Show the landed, all in total cost, and TK still wins on complex tooling while delivering capability domestic shops cannot match on lead time and cavitation.

## Competitive Set

- Alpine Mold

TK's edge against these: a public company with audited financials, a one stop design to production capability, demonstrated brand name programs (Pepsi, Nestlé, Kraft, SC Johnson, Bayer Aleve, mesa Biotech diagnostics), and a US based engineer as the single point of accountability.
