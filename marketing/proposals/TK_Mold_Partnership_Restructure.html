The Shift in the U.S. Mold Market

U.S. OEMs are moving off Asian mold sourcing.

This is not a cyclical reaction to tariffs. It is a durable structural shift that favors toolmakers with U.S. manufacturing capability.

What is driving it

  • Effective tariff stack on Chinese origin molds now 78 to 88 percent (MFN base, Section 301, Section 232 steel derivatives, IEEPA)
  • Reshoring pressure accelerating at the largest OEMs in medical, automotive, packaging, and consumer electronics
  • IP protection concerns intensifying on high value programs
  • Supply chain resilience now a boardroom priority after 2020 to 2024 disruptions
  • Shorter lead time requirements as product lifecycles compress
  • Geopolitical risk priced into every major sourcing decision

The pace is accelerating, not reversing.

Two Acquisition Paths

TK has optionality on how to enter U.S. manufacturing.

Path A  |  Brandon Acquires via SBA

  • Brandon acquires Jaeco personally through HTA
  • SBA 7(a) loan pathway, credit review update expected early May 2026
  • TK accesses U.S. manufacturing through the MoldMinds partnership
  • Lower capital commitment from TK
  • Existing seller relationships carry forward cleanly
  • Brandon operates the asset, TK partners commercially and technically

Path B  |  TK Contracted Acquisition

  • TK contracts Brandon to acquire Jaeco on TK's behalf
  • TK owns the asset directly
  • Brandon operates Jaeco under a management and services agreement
  • Faster close, not gated on SBA timeline
  • Direct TK control of U.S. manufacturing footprint
  • Higher capital commitment from TK with the corresponding return and ownership
Either way, TK gets the footprint. The question for leadership is ownership structure and capital appetite.

Partnership Structure

MoldMinds public. Brandon silent. HTA contract restructured.

Public Partner

MoldMinds.com, a DBA of Henderson Tooling Associates, Inc., operates as TK Mold's partner in the Americas. MoldMinds is the market facing brand. All client relationships, communications, sourcing coordination, and marketing activity run under the MoldMinds name. TK's intellectual property, customer relationships, and brand identity are protected at all times.

Silent Partner

Brandon Henderson operates as silent partner behind MoldMinds, providing strategic direction, technical oversight, and the acquisition pathway for Jaeco. Brandon's name does not appear on customer facing materials.

Foundation

HTA already holds an active contract with TK Mold. This proposal restructures that existing agreement to reflect the expanded scope: agency operations in North America plus the U.S. manufacturing pathway through Jaeco.

Optional brand presence: tkmold.us is already secured. If TK prefers a direct U.S. brand identity, the website will be built to TK brand standards at HTA's expense, at no cost to TK.
